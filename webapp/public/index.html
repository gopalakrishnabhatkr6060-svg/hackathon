📖 Indian Industrial Metrics Dictionary

Average Price Creep:

Measures cumulative contract unit price creep over agreed purchase baseline. Signals vendor margin erosion.

Recent Rejection Ratio:

Delivered defect percentage (0.01 = 1%). Spike flags factory tooling wear or supplier quality degradation.

Stock Cover (Days):

Number of manufacturing days before inventory runs out. Values under 15 days trigger emergency expedited shipping.

Single Source Reliance:

Sole-supplier flag indicating zero qualified backup suppliers. Extreme supply chain fragility vector.

Rejection Spike Ratio:

Ratio of recent lot defect rates against the 12-month trailing moving average.

Component Criticality:

Bill-of-Materials weighting tier (1-5). Tier 4 and 5 components immediately halt finished goods manufacturing.
